An index of award-winning work from ASPLOS, MICRO, ISCA and HPCA, covering awards granted from 2016 onward, inclusive. The archive contains 224 confirmed award records: 113 annual paper awards, 62 long-term impact awards and 49 artifact awards. Each title links to the paper; each record also links to evidence of its award.

The cutoff applies to the award year, not the paper year. A paper published in 2002 and recognized in 2017 belongs here. Both years are shown throughout. The snapshot was checked on October 19, 2026; the coverage notes below identify unresolved results.

What the awards mean

Paper awards include Best Paper, Distinguished Paper, Best Student Paper, and explicitly awarded Runner-up or Honorable Mention distinctions. Names are preserved as published. A Best Paper Candidate, Nominee or Finalist is not treated as a winner.

Long-term impact includes ASPLOS and ISCA Influential Paper awards, and MICRO and HPCA Test of Time awards. These recognize earlier conference papers. Artifact awards recognize research artifacts and appear as a separate category; ordinary artifact evaluation badges are excluded.

ConferenceAnnual paper awardsLong-term recognition
ASPLOSBest Paper; Distinguished Paper in 2021 and 2023; formal secondary distinctionsInfluential Paper, established in 2011; papers from ten or more ASPLOS editions earlier
MICROBest Paper and formal secondary distinctionsTest of Time, established in 2014; the current eligibility window is 18–22 years earlier
ISCABest Paper, established in 2022; formal Honorable Mentions in 2025Influential ISCA Paper, established in 2003; originally a 15-year lookback, now a 20-year window with a two-year allowance on either side
HPCABest Paper; two Distinguished Papers in 2016; formal secondary distinctionsTest of Time, established in 2018; the current eligibility window is 18–22 years earlier

See the official ASPLOS Influential rules, MICRO Test of Time rules, ISCA Best Paper introduction, ISCA Influential rules, and HPCA Test of Time rules.

IEEE Micro Top Picks, HiPEAC Paper Awards, Best of CAL, retrospective collections, presentation prizes and awards to people fall outside this archive.

ASPLOS

Annual paper awards are covered for 2016–2026. The named 2016 winner is verified, but the total for that year is unconfirmed. For 2021, the official award page names six Distinguished Papers while the SIGARCH conference report says seven; the archive retains the six named papers. The claim that OpenPiton received a 2016 Runner-up award remains unconfirmed and is excluded.

Influential awards are covered for 2016–2026. Artifact award records cover 2021–2025; 2020 and 2026 remain unresolved. An artifact's award year can differ from its paper year: RECom is an ASPLOS 2023 paper recognized in 2024.

MICRO

Annual winners and Test of Time awards are covered for 2016–2025. Formal secondary distinctions are included when verified, but their completeness is not guaranteed. The official Test of Time archive provides the long-term award history.

Artifact winners are verified for 2021–2023; 2024–2025 remain unresolved. MICRO 2026 runs from October 31 to November 4, after this snapshot, so this archive contains no 2026 MICRO results.

ISCA

Best Paper winners are covered from the award's introduction in 2022 through 2026, alongside the two formal 2025 Honorable Mentions. Influential awards are covered for 2016–2026. The 2023 award announcement confirms SCALO as a Best Paper co-winner, although the permanent recipients list omits it.

Artifact records cover 2023–2026. The 2025 list contains at least three verified papers and may be incomplete. Some sources call the same recognition Best Artifact and others Distinguished Artifact; these aliases are counted once.

HPCA

Annual paper awards are covered for 2016–2025. HPCA 2026 has already taken place, but its final Best Paper winner could not be verified. The official program identifies Focus, LoCaLUT, RPU and PinDrop as candidates; none is promoted to a confirmed winner here.

The Test of Time archive is covered for 2018–2026, including the 2019 Honorable Mention. Artifact records cover 2022, 2024, 2025 and 2026; the 2023 result remains unresolved.

Sources and paper versions

Award evidence comes primarily from conference websites and sponsoring societies. Author and university announcements fill gaps in those archives. Evidence links are attached to individual records so that each award can be checked independently.

Paper links identify the original conference work. They avoid later journal extensions and retrospective reprints when those would change the cited paper. The paper year refers to the conference edition, which can differ from the publisher's first online date. In particular, Risotto belongs to ASPLOS 2023 even though its DOI metadata shows a 2022 online publication date.
