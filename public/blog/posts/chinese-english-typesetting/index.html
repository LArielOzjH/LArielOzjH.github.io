中英文混排最容易出问题的地方不是字号本身，而是 baseline、字面高度、fallback font、以及 paragraph rhythm。

Goal

The goal is not to make Chinese visually identical to English. The goal is to make both languages feel intentional in the same line.

Practical adjustments

  • choose serif and CJK fallback as a pair
  • avoid synthetic bold
  • test punctuation near Latin words
  • check line breaks on narrow columns
