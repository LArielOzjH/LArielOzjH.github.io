Small batches are useful for debugging, but they can distort the shape of a distributed training profile.

## Observation

When matmuls shrink, fixed collective overhead becomes easier to see. A debug run can therefore overstate the communication problem or hide the optimization that matters at scale.

## Protocol

1. keep one trace at the target batch size
2. keep one trace at the debug batch size
3. write down which conclusions survive both
